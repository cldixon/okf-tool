This library is the checkout team's memory. Our agents write most of it as they work: what they measured, what they found and what we decided. We read it, comment where something is wrong or missing, and mark a page checked once we have read it closely.

- [Metrics](/metrics/checkout-latency.md) say what we measure and why it moved.
- [Decisions](/decisions/retry-budget.md) record what we chose and what we gave up.
- [Runbooks](/runbooks/provider-outage.md) are what to do when something breaks.
- [Research](/research/card-declines.md) is what we learned before deciding.
