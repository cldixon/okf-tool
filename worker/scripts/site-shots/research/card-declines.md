type: Research
title: Why cards are declined at checkout
description: What the decline codes from the card network said over three months, and which declines a retry could have saved.
tags: [payments, research]
---

Over July to September, 3.1% of card checks were declined. Most declines are the buyer's bank saying no, which no retry changes. One in three, though, were timeouts or "try again" answers from the network, and most of those passed on a second or third try.

That finding is why we raised the [retry budget](/decisions/retry-budget.md).
