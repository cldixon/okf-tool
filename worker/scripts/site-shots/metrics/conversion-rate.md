---
type: Metric
title: Checkout conversion
description: The share of buyers who reach the payment step and complete an order, by day and by payment method.
tags: [checkout, conversion]
---

Checkout conversion is completed orders divided by sessions that reached the payment step, per day. It is reported by payment method, because a drop that affects one method points at its provider.

Since the retry change in October, conversion on cards rose from 91.2% to 92.0%: orders that used to fail on a slow answer from the card network now complete on a retry. See [Checkout latency](/metrics/checkout-latency.md) for what that cost in waiting time.
